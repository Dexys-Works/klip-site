From one long video to clips you can post

Three steps. The slow parts happen while you do something else.

    Add your video

    Paste a link or upload a file. Pick a clip length, how many clips and a caption style, or keep the defaults.

  2. 02

    Klip finds the moments

    It transcribes the video, picks the strongest moments and scores each one, with the reasons. Titles, descriptions and hashtags are written for you.

    Edit and export

Questions

How are minutes counted?

By the length of each video you add, once. Finding clips again, editing and exporting don’t use more minutes. Your minutes reset every month.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free includes 30 minutes of video a month, videos up to 20 minutes long, 720p exports and a watermark. No card needed.

Which languages does it understand?

Videos are transcribed in 15 languages, including English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Dutch. Klip detects the language, or you choose it. The app itself is in English, Brazilian Portuguese and Dutch.

Do you train AI on my videos?

No. Your content is used only to make your clips, and our AI providers don’t use it for training either.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU: videos, transcripts, clips and payments. Sign-in runs on Clerk in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Paid plans renew monthly until you cancel from the billing page. You keep your plan until the end of the month you paid for.

Who owns the clips?

You do. You keep all rights to your videos and to the clips you make.
